# Titan Gym Website Unanswered Questions

Use these questions to guide further discussion, customer research, and design tests. Each question includes the website choices its answer could inform. These are possible effects, not predetermined changes. The agreed audience and working direction are in the [design brief](DESIGN_BRIEF.md). Move resolved answers into that brief and update this list as the discussion progresses.

## Resolved

- [x] **Question 1 Walk-in intent:** Approximately 60% seek information and 40% arrive ready to sign up. Encourage in-person visits as the primary website action because they are better for sales; keep online signup secondary. Recorded in the [design brief](DESIGN_BRIEF.md#first-visit).
- [x] **Question 2 Barrier priorities:** The user's current assessment is that price and not knowing Titan exists are probably the biggest visit barriers. Other concerns remain hypotheses rather than established priorities.
- [x] **Question 3 Main discovery source:** Google searches for “gyms near me” are the largest reported driver to the gym.
- [x] **Question 12 Join Now destination:** Scroll immediately to homepage memberships. Show the annual discount and a large message encouraging visitors to ask in person about daily or ongoing specials.

## Customer research

4. **Common questions:** What do prospects most often ask by phone, message, or at the front desk? What information do visitors already have, and which answers do they still need before deciding to visit or join? The user does not know yet; keep for follow-up.

   **What this could change:** Which answers appear on the homepage, membership page, and FAQ, and how prominently. Repeated questions may reveal information that needs clearer wording or earlier placement.

5. **Reasons for choosing Titan:** What do new members say made them choose Titan over other gyms? What nearly stopped them? Nearby competitors and a price difference were discussed, but members' own reasons remain unknown.

   **What this could change:** The headline, main benefits, and supporting testimonials. Member answers could establish whether flexibility, atmosphere, location, equipment, price, or another factor deserves emphasis.

## Operations and offers

6. **Paid coaching:** What coaching services are available, how are they arranged, and what do they cost? Included support is resolved: there is no structured extra support; members can ask an available owner or trainer for informal help. See the design brief.

   **What this could change:** How membership and coaching are separated, which service details and prices appear, and whether a coaching inquiry needs its own action. Prevents implying coaching comes with gym access.

7. **Quiet hours:** Which exact day-by-day windows should be published, and how should the guidance stay current? Partly answered: almost all mornings, much of the daytime before roughly 1–2 p.m., and Sunday afternoons are commonly quieter. Exact times remain unconfirmed. The goal is 50 additional members across the schedule, not exclusively off-peak membership.

   **What this could change:** A recommended-times section, day-by-day attendance guidance, or a simple note near opening hours. It could help visitors plan a less crowded first visit without promising silence or an empty gym.

8. **First visit:** Can the proposed brief guided introduction become a consistent experience? Who provides it, when is it available, and is an appointment needed? The gym is approximately 2,000 square feet, so clarify the value of conversation and guidance rather than assuming an extensive tour.

   **What this could change:** The first-visit explanation and whether the website says to drop in, contact staff, or book. A confirmed process could support specific reassurance about what happens on arrival.

9. **Pass terms and purchase:** What do paid day and week passes cost, how are they purchased, and what conditions apply when crediting their cost toward membership? Paid passes and membership credit are confirmed; there are no free trial workouts.

   **What this could change:** Pass pricing cards, the explanation of membership credit, purchase links, and whether a paid pass is a practical first step to feature.

10. **Annual membership details:** What refund terms and detailed cancellation requirements apply to the prepaid annual membership? Confirmed card wording is “Annually,” “Cancel annually,” and “No enrollment fee.” Monthly terms are resolved: month to month, cancel anytime, and no enrollment fee. Keep the two plans' terms distinct in public copy.

    **What this could change:** The plan comparison, commitment labels, and cancellation FAQ. It determines how to explain the annual option without extending monthly cancellation terms to it.

11. **Children visiting:** Under what conditions can members bring children? What ages, supervision rules, and spaces are permitted? Some members bring children, but there is no childcare service.

    **What this could change:** Practical visit information and a concise FAQ for parents. Establishes what can be stated about bringing children without implying supervision or childcare is provided.

## Design and testing decisions

13. **Color treatment:** Which cream and charcoal shades and accent proportions achieve the intended balance when viewed in actual designs? The approximate 45/45/10 direction remains exploratory.

    **What this could change:** Section backgrounds, text contrast, button treatments, and green accent placement. Reviewing actual layouts will show whether the palette communicates warmth and strength together.

14. **Photo selection:** Which actual photos best support the audience and message? Use placeholders until selection; a collection exists, but its coverage has not been reviewed.

    **What this could change:** The hero image and supporting photos of members, staff, and equipment. Selection should help visitors picture themselves training while showing the gym's actual size and character.

15. **Owners on the homepage:** How prominent should the owners and their story be? The user has deferred this decision.

    **What this could change:** Whether the homepage includes owner portraits and a substantial introduction, a short ownership mention, or a link to a fuller story on the About page.

## Member stories

16. **Approved member stories:** Which actual member experiences, quotes, and photos can be collected and approved for use? The user confirms stories could be obtained. Fictional draft text is permitted with visible replacement labels, as specified in the design brief.

    **What this could change:** The final testimonials, benefits they substantiate, and accompanying portraits. Clearly marked placeholders allow layout work to proceed while actual stories are collected.

## Review after design and copy

- [ ] Review keywords, SEO, and AI SEO after this website design and copy pass. Start with the reported main discovery phrase, “gyms near me,” and evaluate local search visibility and how the gym is represented in AI search results.

  **What this could change:** Page titles, descriptions, local business information, content coverage, and supporting structured information, based on the later review's findings.
